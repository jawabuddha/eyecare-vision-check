Distance vision check

See how sharp your distance vision is with your current glasses or contacts, one eye at a time. Repeat it every few months to spot when it may be time for an eye exam.

What are you wearing for this check?

You’ll need

  • A bank or ID card, to size the letters to your screen
  • A tape measure and a spot about 3 m (10 ft) from the screen
  • A helper to tap your answers, a keyboard, or your voice
  • Good room light, with screen brightness turned up

This is a prototype, not a medical test, and it doesn’t check eye health. See an eye doctor right away for sudden vision loss, new flashes or floaters, a shadow over your vision, or eye pain.

Step 1 of 3

Size your screen

Hold a bank or ID card upright against the screen. Drag the slider until the outline is exactly as wide as the card.

Any standard bank, ID, or loyalty card works. They’re all the same size.

Step 2 of 3

Set up the distance

Prop the screen up at eye level, step back, and measure from the screen to your eyes.

How will you answer?

Tap Test voice and say “left” or “up” to check that the app hears you.

You’ll see one letter E at a time. Answer which way its arms point: up, down, left, or right.

If you can’t make it out, guess. Guesses count. Use “Can’t tell” only when it’s a blur.
